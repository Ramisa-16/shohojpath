Why Shohojpath

The Problem & How We Solve It

Bengali script is uniquely hard for dyslexic readers. Here is why — and what we did about it.

❌ The Problem

  • ✕ বাংলা যুক্তাক্ষর (conjuncts) fuse letters into unrecognisable glyphs like ক্ষ, making decoding impossible for dyslexic readers
  • ✕ Generic reading apps don't understand Bengali script structure — they can't split conjuncts or adjust spacing
  • ✕ Dense text blocks cause visual crowding and eye fatigue, especially for readers with visual stress
  • ✕ No Bengali app offers research-backed reading profiles designed for dyslexia
  • ✕ Parents and teachers have no tool to track reading progress in Bengali

✅ Our Solution

  • ✓ Conjunct splitting — inserts ZWNJ after the hasant (হসন্ত) to break ক্+ষ into visible ক্‌ষ instead of fused ক্ষ
  • ✓ 3 research profiles — Default, Recommended (evidence-based), and Custom. Each is a logged research condition
  • ✓ Full customisation — font, size, spacing, line height, colour themes, reading ruler, focus mode, all adjustable
  • ✓ HCD research prototype — built for a controlled user study with session logging, NASA-TLX, SUS, and quiz feedback
  • ✓ Free & open — no ads, no tracking, just a tool designed to help Bengali readers thrive

The Research

Built on Human-Centered Design

Shohojpath is not just an app — it is a controlled research prototype for a thesis titled:

"Design and Evaluation of a Dyslexia-Friendly Bangla Reading Interface Using Human-Centered Design Principles"

Every settings change is logged with a timestamp and the active research condition, so sessions can be mapped to the independent-variable analysis.
