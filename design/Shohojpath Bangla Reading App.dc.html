A dyslexia-friendly Bangla reading interface. Every screen below is built to answer one question: how can a human-centred Bangla reading interface support decoding for people with dyslexia?

Design assumptions
No design system was available, so the visual language is taken from your slide deck: deep blue #1F4E79, teal #26A69A, cream and light-yellow reading surfaces, Material rounded icons, and 48 px minimum touch targets. Bangla is set in Noto Sans Bengali; UI labels are English. Passages are written sample text — swap in your study material.
Reading profile (research condition)
Switch conditions to compare the control interface against the evidence-based preset and the reader's own settings. The Reading screen re-renders live.
